How to Use

Simple. Ritual. Effective.

01
🥣
Take & Prepare

Take the required amount of Aranya Natural Hair Cleansing Powder in a bowl. Adjust quantity based on your hair length and thickness.

02
💧
Mix to a Paste

Add water gradually and mix well to form a smooth, lump-free paste. You can add rose water or coconut milk for added benefit.

03
✨
Apply & Rinse

Apply to wet hair and scalp. Massage gently to cleanse. Leave for 2–3 minutes, then rinse thoroughly with water.
